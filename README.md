# 🎈 Favorite Movie: *It* (2017)

> *"You'll float too."*

***It*** is a supernatural horror film based on Stephen King's 1986 novel. Set in the small town of Derry, Maine, in 1989, a group of bullied outcasts known as the Losers' Club band together to confront a terrifying, shape-shifting entity that preys on local children and takes the form of Pennywise the Dancing Clown.

## ✨ Why I Love This Movie

1. **Unforgettable Villain:** Bill Skarsgård's eerie physicality and voice as Pennywise create genuine dread in every scene he appears in.
2. **Heartfelt Coming-of-Age Story:** Beyond the scares, the bond and humor shared by the Losers' Club give the movie real emotional depth.
3. **Atmospheric Cinematography:** The 1980s small-town setting, eerie lighting, and practical effects make Derry feel unsettling and alive.
